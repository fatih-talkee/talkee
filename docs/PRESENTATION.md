## Slide 4: How Calls Work

### Title
**How Calls Work**
*Simple. Fast. Secure.*

### Content
**The Call Flow:**

1. **User Initiates Call**
   User finds your profile and taps "Call Now"

2. **You Receive Notification**
   Push notification alerts you of incoming call

3. **Accept or Decline**
   Answer when available, users understand you're busy

4. **Consultation Begins**
   Voice or video call starts, timer tracks duration

5. **Get Paid Automatically**
   Earnings credited to your wallet after call ends
